# Payment Options & Policy

## Supported Payment Methods
1. **UPI**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred.
2. **Credit & Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Maestro, American Express.
3. **Net Banking**: All major Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, etc.).
4. **Digital Wallets**: Amazon Pay, Paytm Wallet, Mobikwik.
5. **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available for orders up to ₹2,500.

## Failed Transactions & Refunds
If money was deducted for a failed order transaction, funds are automatically reversed by your issuing bank within **24-48 hours**.
