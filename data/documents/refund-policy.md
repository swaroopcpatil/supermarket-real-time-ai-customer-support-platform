# Refund Policy

## Overview
At Supermarket Real-Time Support, we aim to ensure complete satisfaction with your purchases. If you receive a damaged, defective, expired, or missing item, you are eligible to request a refund or replacement under the conditions outlined below.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Damaged or Expired Products**: Must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery.
- **Missing Items**: Must be reported within **24 hours** of order delivery.
- **Incorrect Items Delivered**: Must be reported within **48 hours**. Unopened items can be returned or exchanged.
- **Change of Mind**: Perishable goods (dairy, fresh produce, frozen items) cannot be returned due to change of mind. Non-perishable items may be returned within 7 days if unopened and in original packaging.

## Refund Process
1. Contact customer support via our AI Assistant or Support Portal with your Order ID (`ORD-XXXX`).
2. Specify the item(s), quantity, and reason for refund/return (attach proof of damage if applicable).
3. Our AI Refund Agent or Support Team will review the request.
4. Approved refunds will be credited to the original payment method within **3-5 business days** or instantly to Supermarket Wallet credits.

> [!NOTE]
> AI Assistant can initiate refund requests on your behalf (`RFD-XXXX`), but all refunds above ₹500 require manual review by our support desk.
