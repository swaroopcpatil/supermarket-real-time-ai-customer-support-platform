# Supermarket Customer FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
Orders can be cancelled or modified within **10 minutes** of placing them, provided status is "PLACED". Once status is "PACKING" or "OUT_FOR_DELIVERY", items cannot be removed.

### Q: What if an item is out of stock?
If an item goes out of stock after order placement, our system will notify you and auto-refund the item cost.

### Q: How do I request a human support agent?
Simply type *"I want to speak with a human support agent"* or *"Escalate to human support"* in the AI chat window. The assistant will immediately raise a high-priority ticket (`TKT-XXXX`) for immediate agent dispatch.

### Q: Are discounts applicable on sale items?
Yes! Discounted pricing is automatically applied at checkout. You can also ask our AI assistant: *"What products are available under ₹500?"* or *"Which cooking oil is on offer?"*.
