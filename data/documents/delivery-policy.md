# Delivery Policy & Charges

## Express & Standard Delivery
- **Standard Delivery**: Free delivery on all orders above **₹499**. A standard delivery fee of **₹39** applies to orders below ₹499.
- **Express 30-Minute Delivery**: Available for select pin codes for an additional ₹29 fee.
- **Minimum Order Value**: There is no minimum order threshold, but delivery charges apply to smaller orders.

## Delivery Slots
- Deliveries operate daily between **7:00 AM and 10:00 PM**.
- Customers can track live order status ("PACKING", "OUT_FOR_DELIVERY", "DELIVERED") in real time via the app or AI assistant.
