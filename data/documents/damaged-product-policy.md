# Damaged & Spoiled Product Policy

## Reporting Damaged Items
If your package arrives crushed, leaked, broken, or spoiled:
1. Open the AI Assistant or go to your active orders page.
2. Tell the AI: *"My order ORD-XXXX has a damaged item"* or click *Request Refund*.
3. The AI Refund Agent will verify order status and auto-generate a refund request (`RFD-XXXX`).
4. Instant wallet credit or original payment method refund will be issued upon automated validation.
