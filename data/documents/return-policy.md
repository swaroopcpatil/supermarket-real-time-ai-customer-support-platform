# Return Policy

## Return Guidelines
- **Non-Perishable Grocery & Household Items**: Eligible for return within **7 days** of purchase/delivery provided the item is unopened, unused, and in original packaging.
- **Perishable Items (Dairy, Bakery, Meat, Fruits & Vegetables)**: Not eligible for return after delivery acceptance unless damaged or spoiled at the time of delivery.
- **Personal Care Products**: Sealed items can be returned within 3 days. Opened personal care hygiene products are strictly non-returnable.

## Pickup & Exchanges
- Store pickup returns can be processed at any Supermarket customer desk with receipt/order ID.
- Home pickup is free for incorrect or damaged items. For voluntary returns, a nominal ₹30 pickup fee applies.
